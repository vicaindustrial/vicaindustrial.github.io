Tier 1 automotive · Automation and safety retrofit

Band saw automated with servo, PLC and safety guarding

The challenge

The operator worked too close to the moving blade, and test tires could be ejected during the cut. The goal was to remove the operator from the danger zone and increase cutting capacity without compromising the machine's original structure.

The solution

  • Table extended by 500 mm and rebuilt in aluminum, with manual 0–15° tilt for angled cuts.
  • Perimeter guard with an interlocked door that isolates the blade.
  • Servo with ball screw to drive the table, a VFD for the blade motor and a gear reducer to unload the servomotor.
  • Siemens HMI with S7-1200 PLC: feed, blade speed, automatic mode, cut counter, statistics, alarms and emergency stops.

Multinational tire maker · Exported to Canada

Two final tire inspection stations

The challenge

Give the operator an ergonomic station for the final quality inspection of the finished tire.

The solution

  • An Atlas Copco air motor drives a roller and a NEXEN clutch moves the rotating platform.
  • Multilift lifting columns adjust the height of the whole system.
  • A PHD guided cylinder brings the tire toward the operator and another adjusts the roller tilt.
  • Allen-Bradley sensors provide feedback to the control system.

The result

Functional test at our shop with the client's engineer present and remote validation from Canada. Both machines passed on the first test and shipped crated, with nameplates in technical English.

Multinational tire maker · Fabrication and automation · 5 units

Automated foam presses

The challenge

The client had an obsolete foam press and needed to modernize and automate it.

The solution

  • Fabrication of the structure, guarding and mechanical components at our plant.
  • Pneumatic and control integration with a Siemens S7-1200 PLC.
  • Safety system with light curtains, operator station and emergency stop.
  • Commissioning and functional testing at our plant before every shipment.

The result

5 presses delivered: 4 to the client's plants in Mexico's Bajío region and 1 exported to the United States. All were approved on their first acceptance test.
